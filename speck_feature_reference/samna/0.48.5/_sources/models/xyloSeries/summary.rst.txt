###########
Xylo Series
###########

The **Xylo** series features fully digital AI chips designed with Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) and
Reservoir Computing technology. These chips are versatile and capable of supporting a wide range of applications,
including keyword spotting (KWS), electrocardiogram (ECG) anomaly detection, vibration anomaly detection,
and ambient sound detection. With the right network configuration and training, Xylo chips can deliver powerful,
efficient solutions for these and other tasks.
