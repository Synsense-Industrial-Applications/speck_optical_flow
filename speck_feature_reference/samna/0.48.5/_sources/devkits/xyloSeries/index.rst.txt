###########
Xylo Series
###########

The Xylo series consists of different chip variants designed for low-power sensing applications.
These chips are provided in development kits, which include external sensors integrated with the hardware,
allowing for versatile development in areas such as audio processing, motion detection
and environmental monitoring.
