
Abbreviations
=============

+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Abbreviation** | **Full Name**                                                     |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| ACC              | Access                                                            |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| bits             | Built-In Self Test SRAM Interface. Used for SPI/JTAG SRAM access. |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BL               | Burst Length                                                      |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BM               | Burst Mode                                                        |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BPI              | Bus Peripheral Interface                                          |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BRAM             | Bias RAM                                                          |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| E2I              | External to Internal                                              |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| FRAM             | Filter RAM                                                        |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| I2E              | Internal to External                                              |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| IM               | Instruction Mode                                                  |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| KRAM             | Kernel RAM                                                        |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| LBM              | Long Burst Mode                                                   |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| NRAM             | Neuron RAM                                                        |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SBM              | Short Burst Mode                                                  |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SM               | Single Mode                                                       |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SPI              | Serial Periphery Interface                                        |
+------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
